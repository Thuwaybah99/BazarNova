01 / CONTEXT

The business story

BazarNova's 2017 retail records offer an opportunity to move from descriptive reporting to a clearer picture of commercial performance.

Rather than treating sales as one number, this analysis connects time, product hierarchy, promotions and store location to examine where sales are concentrated and which patterns deserve business attention.

This portfolio case study is based on the project’s documented dataset and original analysis. Findings are descriptive associations—not proof of cause or profit impact.

05 / SYNTHESIS

What the evidence suggests

Four takeaways drawn from the project's published analysis—not new calculations from the live dashboards.

01DEMAND

Year-end demand deserves an earlier response.

The project identifies December as the strongest month and describes a late-year acceleration. This points to the value of planning stock and campaigns ahead of the peak.

02PRODUCT MIX

High volume ≠ high revenue.

H00 leads in recorded sales volume; H01 is especially important in the revenue picture. Assortment choices should consider both.

03PROMOTIONS

Campaign results are uneven.

Promotion 14 dominates the reported campaign results, whereas several other promotions are much weaker and warrant review.

04STORES

Performance is concentrated.

DIGI and Electro World (National) lead the stores studied, with category mix a possible contributor to the differences.

!

How far can these findings go?

Only six of the 144 stores cited in the project were included. Differences in sales may reflect store size, assortment or coverage; revenue is not profit. The original README includes some percentage statements whose denominators should be checked before treating them as final KPIs. Recommendations below are hypotheses to test, not proven outcomes.

06 / DECISION-MAKING

From findings to actions

Prioritized recommendations that connect observed patterns to practical next steps.

01 / PLAN

Prepare earlier for the year-end peak

Use prior seasonal patterns to test inventory, replenishment and campaign schedules ahead of Q4.

Demand readiness ↗
02 / OPTIMIZE

Manage categories for both volume and value

Review H00 and H01 together; track sales, revenue and—if cost data becomes available—gross margin by hierarchy.

Better product mix ↗
03 / EXPERIMENT

Test why Promotion 14 outperformed

Compare campaign audience, offer, product mix and timing before replicating it. Evaluate incremental lift where a control is possible.

Smarter campaigns ↗
04 / EXPAND

Investigate store drivers and widen coverage

Compare like-for-like stores and incorporate more locations before making network-wide operational changes.
